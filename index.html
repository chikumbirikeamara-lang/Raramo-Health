ABOUT RARAMO HEALTH

Supporting women beyond the pregnancy test.

Pregnancy is one of the most significant experiences in a woman's life. Yet navigating it can be overwhelming, particularly when reliable information, timely guidance and access to healthcare services are difficult to obtain.

Raramo Health is an emerging digital health initiative being developed to make maternal health information more accessible, understandable and actionable.

Our vision is to create a digital companion that supports women throughout their maternal journey, from early pregnancy through childbirth and the postpartum period.

We believe that women should not have to navigate pregnancy alone or wait until a health concern becomes an emergency before knowing where to seek help.

THE CHALLENGE

Pregnancy is a journey. Navigating it shouldn't be a struggle.

For many women, particularly in resource-constrained settings, the journey through pregnancy comes with challenges that extend beyond clinical care.

📚

Information Gaps

Women may struggle to find reliable, understandable and relevant information about pregnancy, maternal health and potential complications.

⏰

Delayed Recognition

Limited awareness of maternal danger signs can make it difficult for women and their families to recognise when urgent medical attention is needed.

🏥

Disconnected Care

Knowing when to seek help is only part of the challenge. Identifying appropriate healthcare facilities and navigating referrals can also be difficult.

💬

Limited Continuous Support

Pregnancy brings new questions and concerns at every stage. Women need support that extends beyond occasional clinic visits.

🌍

Access Inequalities

Geographical barriers, limited resources and differences in health literacy can affect women's ability to access maternal health information and services.

❤️

The Postpartum Gap

Maternal support should not end at delivery. The postpartum period also requires information, follow-up and support for mothers.

OUR APPROACH

One digital companion. A more connected maternal health journey.

Raramo Health aims to bring essential elements of maternal health support into one accessible digital experience.

Rather than simply providing pregnancy information, the platform is being designed to help women understand their changing needs, recognise potential warning signs and navigate available healthcare services.

Through a combination of digital education, AI-enabled assistance, reminders and healthcare navigation, Raramo seeks to bridge the gap between information and appropriate care-seeking.

The platform is envisioned for use through a mobile application, with complementary access through channels such as WhatsApp and SMS to accommodate different levels of digital access.

A more connected maternal healthcare experience.

Raramo's proposed referral and healthcare navigation component recognises that maternal health outcomes depend not only on awareness, but also on the ability to access appropriate services.

By connecting women with information about healthcare facilities, available services and appropriate care pathways, Raramo seeks to reduce the distance between recognising a concern and seeking help.

The long-term vision is to develop locally relevant healthcare directories and referral partnerships, beginning with the needs and realities of communities in our target markets.

THE PERSON BEHIND RARAMO

Amara Chikumbirike

Founder, Raramo Health

Amara Chikumbirike is a medical student, public health advocate and social impact founder passionate about improving health outcomes and expanding opportunities for women and girls.

She is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB) at the University of Lusaka in Zambia. Her medical training has strengthened her interest in the intersection of clinical care, public health and the role of innovation in addressing healthcare challenges.

Beyond medical school, Amara is the founder of Girl Up Lusaka, a youth-led initiative focused on menstrual health, girls' education, sexual and reproductive health, mentorship and gender equality. Through this work, she has contributed to initiatives reaching more than 1,000 girls.

Her experience in community health advocacy and engagement with underserved communities has shaped her interest in solutions that address the barriers people experience when accessing essential health information and services.

Raramo Health reflects her growing interest in digital health and maternal health innovation. She envisions a platform that combines accessible health education, responsible artificial intelligence and healthcare navigation to support women through pregnancy and beyond.

Through Raramo, Amara hopes to contribute to a future in which technology strengthens the connection between women, communities and healthcare systems.
